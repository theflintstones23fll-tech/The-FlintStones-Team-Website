Innovation Project

FlintAI

Our Solution to Archaeological Fragmentation

The Problem

Fragments Lost in Time

Archaeologists excavating ancient sites routinely recover thousands of pottery shards, stone fragments, and broken artifacts — but lack effective systematic methods to match and reassemble them.

  • ⚠ Thousands of fragments per excavation site
  • ⚠ Manual matching takes months or years
  • ⚠ Institutional knowledge lost when experts retire
  • ⚠ Storage degradation damages fragments further
Our Solution

AI-Powered Fragment Matching

FlintAI is a web platform that uses computer vision and machine learning to analyze photographs of artifact fragments, classify them by structural characteristics and suggest fragments with high matching potential.

📸
Photo Analysis

Upload fragment photos; AI extracts shape, texture, and edge data

🧠
Smart Classification

CNN model classifies fragments by period, material, and form

🔗
Match Suggestions

Similarity scoring ranks candidate matches from database

🗄
Digital Archive

Preserve fragments digitally for future reconstruction

About the Team

We are The Flintstones — a passionate FLL team named after the enduring spirit of those who built civilization from stone.

Founded at Özel Çakabey Okulları under coach Oğuzhan Köse — who has led teams to the Asia-Pacific World Championship over 16 years — we placed 3rd at regionals, then conquered the National Tournament, beating 74 teams from 21 cities across Turkey.
